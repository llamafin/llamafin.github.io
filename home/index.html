Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Llamafin?

Llamafin is a cross-platform music player that connects to your Jellyfin media server. It provides a premium listening experience with professional audio processing, offline downloads, DJ mode, remote device control, and much more — all completely free.

Do I need a Jellyfin server?

Yes. Llamafin is designed to work with a self-hosted Jellyfin media server. Jellyfin is a free, open-source media server that you can install on your own hardware. Llamafin connects to your server to stream your personal music collection.

Is Llamafin really free? What's the catch?

Yes, Llamafin is 100% free with no subscriptions, no premium tiers, and no ads. It's built for the Jellyfin community. There is no catch — Great music software shouldn't cost a fortune (or anything at all).

What platforms does Llamafin support?

Llamafin is available on Android (Google Play), iOS (sideload), Windows, macOS and Linux. That's 5 platforms with a consistent, high-quality experience everywhere.

How do I install Llamafin on iOS?

iOS installation requires sideloading since we don't have a paid Apple Developer account (this is a free app, and paying Apple yearly doesn't make sense). Visit our Download page for step-by-step instructions on sideloading the IPA file using tools like AltStore or Sideloadly.

Can I use Llamafin offline?

Absolutely. Llamafin has best-in-class offline support. Download your music with smart quality selection, and enjoy your entire library without an internet connection. Offline search, and offline library browsing all work seamlessly.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Llamafin connects to your own Jellyfin server — no third-party servers, no tracking, no analytics. P2P connections use ECDH key exchange and AES-256-GCM encryption. Llamafin doesn't collect, store, or sell any of your personal data. Read the Privacy Policy and Security Summaryfor full details.

Is Llamafin open source?

No. Llamafin is a closed-source application. However, it is built to work with Jellyfin, which is open source. All documentation, feature descriptions, and security details are available right here on this site for full transparency.
