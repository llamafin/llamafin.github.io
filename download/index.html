🍎 iOS (iPhone & iPad)

Due to Apple's $99/year developer program requirement, Llamafin is available via sideloading. This keeps the app free for everyone. Download the IPA below.

Step 1 — Download the IPA:

Download Llamafin IPA

Step 2 — Sideload:

  1. Install a sideloading tool: Choose one of these trusted tools:
    • AltStore (Recommended): https://altstore.io
    • Sideloadly: https://sideloadly.io
  2. Install AltStore/Sideloadly: Follow the tool's installation guide for your computer (macOS or Windows).
  3. Sideload the IPA: Connect your iOS device, open the tool, and drag-and-drop the Llamafin IPA file to install it.
  4. Trust the Developer: On your iOS device, go toSettings → General → VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID developer certificate.
  5. Refresh Every 7 Days: Free developer certificates expire every 7 days. AltStore can auto-refresh when on the same WiFi as your computer.

Note: If you have a paid Apple Developer account, you can self-sign the IPA for a 1-year certificate. Contact us for the provisioning profile.
