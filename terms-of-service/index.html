2. Description of Service

Llamafin is a cross-platform music player application that connects to self-hosted Jellyfin media servers. The App provides features including but not limited to:

  • Music streaming from your Jellyfin server
  • Offline download and playback
  • Professional audio processing (equalizer, stereo width, loudness normalization)
  • DJ Mode and auto-playlist generation
  • Multi-room synchronised playback
  • Peer-to-peer device connectivity (Llamafin Connect)
  • Sonic analysis and audio fingerprinting
  • Music library browsing and search
