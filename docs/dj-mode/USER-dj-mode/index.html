DJ Mode (Guest DJ)

Audience: End users and technical evaluators Last Updated: 2026-04-05 Version: 2.0.0

Overview​

DJ Mode automatically extends your playback queue with intelligent track selection based on your current listening context. Choose from multiple DJ personalities, each with unique music selection strategies. Let Llamafin take the wheel and discover music that matches your mood, artist, or era.

Key Concepts​

What is DJ Mode?​

DJ Mode is your personal auto-DJ that:

  • Monitors what you're currently playing
  • Automatically adds tracks when your queue is running low
  • Uses smart selection strategies based on your chosen DJ personality
  • Remembers your preference across sessions

How It Works​

The Flow:

  1. You select a DJ personality from the Guest DJ menu
  2. You start playing music normally
  3. As each track plays, DJ Mode evaluates the context
  4. When your queue has 5 or fewer tracks remaining, DJ generates 5 new tracks
  5. New tracks are seamlessly appended to your queue
  6. Playback continues without interruption
  7. Process repeats when queue nears end again

Track Markers:

  • DJ-generated tracks are internally flagged for identification
  • Visible in your queue with a special indicator
  • Count toward your play statistics
  • Treated the same as manually-added tracks

How DJ Mode Works​

Activation​

Setting Your DJ Mode:

  1. While playing music, tap the disc icon in the playback controls
  2. The Guest DJ menu opens showing all available DJ personalities
  3. Tap any DJ to activate it immediately
  4. Your selection is saved automatically
  5. The menu closes and DJ Mode is active

Your Choice is Remembered:

  • DJ mode is saved across app sessions
  • Automatically restored when you reopen the app
  • No need to re-select each time
  • Easy to change whenever you want

Track Generation​

When DJ Mode Triggers:

DJ Mode activates automatically when:

  • Queue has 5 or fewer tracks remaining
  • DJ mode is active (not "No Guest DJ")
  • Currently playing track provides context for selection

How Each DJ Selects Tracks:

DJ Groupie​

Strategy: Similar tracks

  • Analyses the current track's characteristics
  • Finds sonically similar songs
  • Uses Jellyfin's similarity algorithm
  • Considers genre, artist, tags, and musical properties
  • Result: Discover related music you might not have found otherwise

DJ Twofer​

Strategy: More from the same artist

  • Identifies the artist of the current track
  • Fetches additional songs by the same artist
  • "Two for one" - double the artist experience
  • Result: Deep dive into your favorite artists' discographies

DJ Contempo​

Strategy: Same era/year

  • Extracts the release year from the current track
  • Finds songs from the same time period
  • "Contemporary" music from the same era
  • Result: Immersive decade-specific listening experience

Queue Extension​

What Happens Behind the Scenes:

  1. DJ analyses the current playing track
  2. Selects 5 tracks matching the DJ's strategy
  3. Appends tracks to the end of your current queue
  4. Marks the queue as "autoplay" mode
  5. Playback continues seamlessly
  6. Process repeats when queue nears end again

Smart Behaviour:

  • DJ works alongside album autoplay
  • DJ takes priority when active
  • Both extend your queue automatically
  • You can still manually add tracks anytime

Use Cases​

Party Mode - Keep the Music Going​

Setup:

  1. Enable DJ Groupie mode
  2. Start with a diverse playlist
  3. Let DJ extend automatically

Result:

  • Continuous varied music
  • Discovers similar content
  • No manual queue management
  • Keeps energy flowing all night

Artist Exploration - Deep Dive​

Setup:

  1. Play any song by your favorite artist
  2. Enable DJ Twofer mode
  3. Let DJ find more tracks

Result:

  • Deep dive into discography
  • Discover lesser-known tracks
  • Comprehensive artist view
  • Automatic sequencing through their work

Time Travel Listening - Era Immersion​

Setup:

  1. Play a track from a specific era (e.g., 70s rock)
  2. Enable DJ Contempo mode
  3. DJ finds same-period music

Result:

  • Immersive decade experience
  • Contextual listening
  • Period-authentic playlists
  • Discover contemporary artists

Casual Listening - Set and Forget​

Setup:

  1. Enable your preferred DJ mode
  2. Play any single track
  3. Let DJ build the queue

Result:

  • Music plays continuously
  • No queue management needed
  • Discover new music automatically
  • Perfect for background listening

Configuration​

Accessing DJ Settings​

From the Player:

  1. Tap the disc icon in playback controls
  2. Guest DJ menu opens
  3. Select your preferred DJ personality

From Settings:

  • Navigate to Settings to view DJ preferences
  • View current active mode
  • Change mode as needed

Understanding DJ Mode Options​

No Guest DJ:

  • DJ Mode is disabled
  • Your queue plays exactly as you built it
  • No automatic track generation
  • Best for: Carefully crafted playlists, album listening

Implemented Modes (Groupie, Twofer, Contempo):

  • Fully functional and ready to use
  • Each uses different selection strategies
  • Try each to find your favorite

Planned Modes (Stretch, Gemini, Freeze):

  • Shown in the menu but disabled
  • Tap shows "coming soon" message
  • Will be enabled in future updates

Integration with Other Features​

Player & Queue​

  • DJ Mode integrates directly with the playback queue
  • Automatically extends queue when running low
  • Works alongside album autoplay
  • DJ takes priority when both are active
  • Generated tracks marked for identification

Library Management​

  • DJ queries your full music library
  • Uses Songs feature APIs for track discovery
  • Respects library permissions and access
  • Works with all your accessible content

Sonic Analysis (Future)​

  • Planned DJ modes (Gemini, Freeze) will use sonic analysis
  • Audio fingerprinting for similarity detection
  • More intelligent track selection
  • Enhanced musical matching

Settings System​

  • DJ mode preference saved automatically
  • Persists across app sessions
  • No additional configuration needed
  • Simple one-tap selection

Error Handling & Reliability​

What Happens When Things Go Wrong​

DJ Generation Failure:

  • Silently handled in the background
  • No interruption to current playback
  • Logged for debugging
  • Your queue continues playing normally

Unimplemented Mode Selected:

  • Shows "coming soon" message
  • No action taken
  • Mode remains disabled
  • Easy to understand status

Network Issues:

  • DJ requires internet for track generation
  • If offline, DJ cannot generate new tracks
  • Your existing queue continues playing
  • Try again when reconnected

Self-Healing Features​

  • Automatic retry: DJ attempts generation on next track change
  • Graceful degradation: Queue continues even if DJ fails
  • State persistence: Your mode selection is always remembered
  • Validation checks: DJ validates all inputs before generation
